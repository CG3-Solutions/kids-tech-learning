# Typing course (Keyo the Keyboard Cat)

Touch typing for kids **and** adults: typing without looking at the keyboard, with every key pressed by the right finger. It has its own area in Spark Lab (⌨️ Typing).

## Two modes
| Mode | Who it's for | What's different |
|---|---|---|
| 🐱 **Kids mode** | 1st–5th standard (default) | Keyo the cat explains each lesson aloud, big letters, a progress bar instead of a timer, and lessons unlock in order. |
| ⌨️ **Pro mode** | 6th standard and up, adults (default from 6th) | A clean screen with live speed and accuracy. Every lesson is open, so you can skip what you already know. |

Anyone can switch modes at the top of the course page. Each mode has its own goals (see below).

## How a lesson works
1. **Intro:** what the new keys are and which fingers press them (Kids mode reads it aloud).
2. **Typing:** the next key glows on the on-screen keyboard, and the hands below show which finger to use. Keys are coloured by finger: little 🟣, ring 🔵, middle 🟢, pointer 🟡.
3. **Mistakes:** a wrong key flashes red and the learner must press the right key to move on. Accuracy counts every wrong press.
4. **Results:** speed (words a minute), accuracy, time, stars and the keys to practise.

**Measuring speed:** one "word" is 5 keys, the standard typing measure. Time counts from the first key. A pause longer than 3 seconds counts as 3 seconds, and time with the tab hidden doesn't count at all.

**Stars:** ⭐ pass, ⭐⭐ 95% accuracy, ⭐⭐⭐ 98% accuracy.

## The lessons (release 1: stages 1–3)
| Stage | Lessons | Goal to pass |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Get ready** | 1 Sit like a typist (F, J, space) · 2 Home row fingers | Kids 85% · Pro 90% accuracy |
| **2. The home row** | 3 D and K · 4 S and L · 5 A and ; · 6 G and H · 7 Home row words · 8 **Home row check** | Check: 90% accuracy and Kids 5 / Pro 15 words a minute |
| **3. The top row** | 9 E and I · 10 R and U · 11 T and Y · 12 W and O · 13 Q and P · 14 Top row words · 15 **Top row check** | Check: 90% accuracy and Kids 8 / Pro 20 words a minute |

The text is new every time and uses **only keys learned so far**: drills of the new keys, then real words (including Indian words such as *dal*, *roti*, *diwali*, *goa*).

Badges: ⌨️ **Home row hero** (pass lesson 8) and 🚀 **Top row ace** (pass lesson 15). Parents get a milestone email for each.

## Tips for parents and teachers
- **Accuracy first, speed later.** A child who types slowly and correctly will get fast; one who rushes builds bad habits.
- **Short and often:** 10 minutes a day is better than an hour on Sunday.
- **Posture:** back straight, feet flat, wrists floating above the keyboard, eyes on the screen.
- **No peeking:** if eyes keep dropping to the keys, cover the hands with a light cloth.
- **Fingers go home:** after every key, the finger returns to the home row (A S D F, J K L ;).
- **Caps Lock:** if it's on, the screen says so instead of counting mistakes.
- **Tablets:** touch typing needs a real keyboard. A Bluetooth keyboard works. Without one, kids can tap the on-screen keys to learn where they are; tapped lessons don't count as speed records.

## Where parents see progress
**Parent area → Progress reports → Typing:** best speed, recent accuracy, typing time, the keys missed most often, and the last lessons. Typing lessons also appear in the activity feed.
