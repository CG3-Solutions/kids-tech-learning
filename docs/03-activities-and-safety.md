# Activities, Machines and Safety

## ⚠️ Safety rule (repeat every time)
- We learn with **batteries only**.
- **Never** touch wall sockets, open chargers, or open appliances that are plugged in.
- Always ask an adult before opening anything.

## How machines are built: a robot is like a person
| Machine part | Like our… | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Sensors | Eyes, ears, skin | Take information in |
| Microcontroller | Brain | Decides what to do |
| Motors, lights, buzzer | Hands, face, voice | Do the action |
| Battery | Food | Gives energy |
| Wires | Nerves | Connect everything |

### Automatic street light
Turns itself on in the evening.

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Light sensor | sees it’s getting dark |
| Microcontroller | thinks: dark? switch ON |
| Transistor / relay | does the switching |
| LEDs | glow |
| Wires | connect it all |

### Washing machine
Washes clothes step by step.

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Buttons | you choose a mode |
| Water sensor | checks the water level |
| Microcontroller | follows the washing steps |
| Motor | spins the drum |
| Buzzer | beeps when done |
| Display | shows time left |

### Car parking sensor
Beeps faster as you get close.

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Distance sensor | measures how close the wall is |
| Microcontroller | closer? beep faster! |
| Buzzer | beeps |

### Toy robot car
Drives around and avoids walls.

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Battery | gives energy |
| Microcontroller | the brain |
| Distance sensor | sees obstacles |
| Motors | turn the wheels |
| LEDs | glowing eyes |
| Buzzer | robot sounds |

## Circuit play (in the app's "Build a circuit" tab, or with Snap Circuits)
1. Flip the switch: the loop closes and the bulb glows. Open it and the light goes off.
2. Swap the bulb for a **motor**: it spins.
3. Swap for a **buzzer**: it beeps.
4. Swap for an **LED**, then turn it around: it won't glow backwards (a one-way street).

## Binary cards (how computers count)
Make 5 cards with 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 dots. Face-up = ON (1), face-down = OFF (0). Make numbers by adding the face-up dots. For example, 5 = 4 + 1. This is how transistors count.

## "Robot parent" game (programming without a screen)
He gives you step-by-step instructions ("2 steps forward, turn right") to reach a toy. Follow them exactly, even when they're wrong, so he finds and fixes the "bug".
